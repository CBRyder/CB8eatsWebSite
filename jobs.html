Jobs

Formal employment — the work you clock in and out of. Route work and world-harvest are deliberately kept separate from this: gathering, racing, and the rest of the open activities live on their own page.

LinkedUp

The phone's job board. Every legal business's entry-level opening auto-appears here the moment it's registered, alongside four flavor jobs — miner, fabricator, mechanic, trucker — that exist for identity, not mechanics. Applying to a real business creates a pending application; the owner reviews and hires from the Applicants tab in Biz Manager.

🔧

Legal & Helper Careers

Mechanic and EMS are built as deep as any illegal path, not afterthoughts. Mechanics diagnose, repair, customize, and bill customers on the spot. EMS handles on-scene revives and hospital respawns, with a real duty toggle gating who's actually on the clock.

⏱️

Time Clock

A phone app any employed player uses to clock on or off duty — business staff, cops, and EMS alike. Being on duty is what actually gates a real paycheck from qbx_core's payroll loop, not just being hired.

💼

Working For Someone

Every business runs on a grade ladder — Employee, Manager, Owner by default, wages set per grade by whoever owns the place. Getting hired means joining that ladder, not just picking up shifts.
