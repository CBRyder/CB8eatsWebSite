Origins

A ritual, a demon, and a bloodline born from both.

The Valkory trace their existence to Reyes Ludorix, a mortal who performed a ritual known as the Calling of the Vor — a precise geometric circle of blood, completed with ash wood, charcoal, and his own vitae. The ritual was answered by Vesh'kal, an ancient demon of balance and twilight: his power is what transformed Reyes into the first Valkory, marking him — and every Valkory after — with red, blood-dark skin and a prehensile tail, and granting the clan's signature ability, Stride. Between them, ritual and demon opened a refuge world previously unknown to any mortal: Asanha. The Valkory who followed Reyes made Altheria their home there, having never truly belonged among the mortal, medieval plane they left behind.

The Gift of Crimson Resonance

Valkory don't simply feed on blood — they absorb the memories, skills, and emotions of whoever they feed on, through a process called Crimson Resonance. It's a genuine advantage, letting a Valkory temporarily draw on knowledge and ability that was never their own — but it comes with real risk: being overwhelmed by memories and personalities that aren't theirs.

The Crimson Codex

The principles that govern the ethical use of Crimson Resonance, and keep the clan from losing themselves in the memories of others.

  • "Feed with purpose, not merely hunger."
  • "Honour the memories thou dost partake."
  • "Let not foreign thoughts overshadow thine own essence."
  • "Share knowledge gained, lest wisdom be lost."

Paths of Power

Valkory abilities aren't predictable — they're shaped by which skill trees a fledgling commits themselves to. A scholar might lean into Scholarly Pursuit and Resonance Mastery; a former warrior into Physical Prowess and Shadow Weaving.

Shadow Weaving

Manipulation of darkness, illusion, and stealth.

Resonance Mastery

Enhanced feeding abilities and memory control.

Physical Prowess

Combat forms, enhanced strength, and tail combat techniques.

Scholarly Pursuit

Knowledge absorption, ancient lore, and mystical research.

Social Grace

Court intrigue, persuasion, and leadership abilities.

Crimson Arts

Blood magic and life force manipulation.

Clan Culture

The Valkory prize intellectual discourse, strategic thinking, and the pursuit of knowledge their gift makes possible. They see themselves as learned guardians, favoring careful analysis over brute force — though they're formidable when cornered. In battle, they favor the khopesh, a curved sickle-sword that's become the clan's signature weapon. That said, the clan isn't free of its own internal struggles; as with any powerful bloodline, there are those within it who seek to challenge the status quo.

The Curse

Power this significant doesn't come free.

  • Light-bound Phantom Stride — Valkory cannot teleport in areas of bright illumination, limiting the ability to shadow and darkness.
  • Tail dependency — severe damage to a Valkory's prehensile tail can sever their connection to their supernatural abilities until it fully heals.
  • Resonance Overflow — when overwhelmed by strong emotion or stress, absorbed memories and feelings from past victims can surge forth and temporarily override a Valkory's own personality.
  • Ancestral Whispers — the voices and thoughts of the consumed echo during moments of concentration or meditation, blurring the line between a Valkory's own thoughts and the mental echoes of the dead.
