Story

Story

A newly-embraced fledgling wakes into the Valkory bloodline, and into Asanaha, three nights turned. What he does with that is still being written.

In Development

The Awakening

"Welcome to thy true life, young fledgling. The blood of the Valkory doth now course through thy veins. Thou shalt learn to step between the spaces of the world, to command shadows as others command light. Yet first, thou must understand what thou hast become, and choose thy path wisely." — Elyonwen

Three nights ago, Tyberius Drake was a mortal of twenty years, born on the rare day of February 29th, 1288. Now, in the year 1309, he is Valkory — awakened in the sprawling realm of Asanaha, with his sire Elyonwen standing before him. His transformation brings abilities that defy the natural order: the power to step through shadow and emerge elsewhere, and the beginning of a hunger that will define the rest of his existence.

Tyberius Drake

Before his embrace, Tyberius was a scholar and antiquarian with expertise in medieval artifacts — analytical, determined, and driven by a thirst for forbidden knowledge, having pursued lore that eventually connected him to the Valkory bloodline itself. His academic mind proved unusually resilient to the shock of transformation, letting him keep much of his human personality and memories intact. As a fledgling he's curious and reserved in equal measure, adaptable to his new circumstances, and once committed to a course, rarely one to abandon it — though he's still working out whether he's chasing knowledge, revenge, or simply a place he finally belongs.

Those Around Him

Elyonwen

Tyberius's sire — a Valkory Elder embraced in 1147, now Shadow Duchess of the Southern Territories. Calculating yet nurturing toward her progeny, she trains fledglings by helping them discover their own path rather than forcing one on them.

Elder Seraphine

A Council member who takes interest in Tyberius's development, seeing political usefulness in his unique perspective. More progressive than most elders on fledglings' rights — offers protection and knowledge, but rarely without a price.

Kara

Assigned to monitor Tyberius, and initially hostile to him. Skeptical of whether he's worthy of the Valkory blood at all, and far ahead of him in the combat side of Phantom Stride.

Elena

A fellow fledgling, embraced around the same time as Tyberius. Adapts more naturally to vampire abilities than he does, but lacks his knowledge — and shares his sense of displacement.

Story Arc

Player choices determine Tyberius's ultimate fate, but his journey follows a general shape.

  • Act 1: Awakening — coming to terms with the transformation, learning basic vampire abilities, first contact with Valkory society, and discovering Asanaha exists at all.
  • Act 2: Trials — proving his worth to Valkory elders, uncovering plots within vampire society, and confronting the truth behind his own embrace.
  • Act 3: Ascension — mastering advanced Valkory abilities, choosing final allegiances, and becoming either savior or conqueror.
