Tarboro Life

A custom, Backstreets-inspired FiveM roleplay server built on QBox. A systems-first RP server where progression, ownership, and crafting loops are the core fun — a voice-driven world with a deeply interlocking economy and newcomer-friendly onboarding. Roleplay emerges from the systems, rather than being performed on top of them.

Core Systems

Every system on the server, at a glance.

  • Economy — middle-ground, less-grind; high earning ceiling balanced by property as the big money sink. Cars run $2k–$2M+, property $10k–$2M+; the millions tier is gated behind stocks and empire-building rather than job-grind.
  • Jobs — route work as formal jobs, world-harvest as open activities; owning a business doesn't mean operating it yourself.
  • Legal & Helper Careers — mechanic, EMS, and social careers are as deep as crime, with plenty of fun to be had without ever committing one.
  • Communication — voice-first via pma-voice; text is de-emphasized to commands and NPC interaction.
  • Phone & App Suite — arrow-key access; the hub for the full in-game app platform.
  • Stock Market — parody and server-specific stocks with drift, event spikes, and an insider-trading app with guardrails.
  • Property — custom interiors, buy or rent, with prefab commission builds fulfilled by admins.
  • Garages — physical, breachable, tiered security, with cameras that report to police.
  • Theft & Insurance — permanent car theft, balanced by a four-tier insurance ladder based on mod coverage.
  • Crime & Consequence — card-first money, downed/bleed-out states, lien garnishment, and legality-based confiscation.
  • Survival Needs — functional hunger and thirst systems that give the food economy real teeth.
  • Crafting Economy — survival gathering, into industrial production, into gunsmithing/fabrication as a deep endgame pillar.

⚠ What this audit actually found

  • tarboro-injuries — bleeding / broken limbs / concussion / sickness, fully built, extends tarboro-death — is not in server.cfg at all. It's never loaded, ever. Confirmed by diffing every folder on disk against every `ensure` line — this was the only mismatch.
  • tarboro-arrival — a genuinely substantial 8-beat cinematic new-player intake sequence (establishing shot, voice narration, walk-to-intake objective, an admin interview window with a Discord webhook, skip/release/reset admin commands) — had zero record anywhere in memory until this pass, despite running live on every fresh character.
  • tarboro-tutorial — the soft "Guided Start" onboarding tracker — same story, built and running, previously undocumented.
  • Five more resources (tarboro-interact, tarboro-hud, tarboro-death, tarboro-requests, tarboro-clothing) are only known indirectly — referenced constantly as integration points by other systems, but with no dedicated build record of their own.
