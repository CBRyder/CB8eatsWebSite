Structure

  • Fixed 4-grade ladder — Member, Enforcer, Shot Caller, Boss. Every gang gets the same ranks; no per-gang customization the way legal businesses can set their own grades.
  • Gang funds — a shared bank, deposit/withdraw, bank authority reserved for the Boss rank.
  • Roster management — invite, kick, and promote/demote members without leaving the phone.

Turf — 7 Zones

Claim a zone by walking up and interacting with it. A rival gang can contest and flip ownership, with a cooldown that stops instant back-and-forth ping-pong over the same block.
