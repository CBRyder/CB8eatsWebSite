Tarboro Life

Businesses & Orgs

Owning a business means putting up the capital and running the books — not clocking in yourself. Buy a commercial property, register what it is, and it starts generating its own economy: staff, payroll, and a shared fund that's genuinely yours to manage.

Nine Types, One Registration Flow

Buying the property and declaring what it is are two separate steps. Picking Illegal opens two more menus instead of one.

  • Legal (7 types) — Automotive, Hair/Nail, Clothes, Dispensary, Pawn, Diner, Coffee.
  • Mining / Resource Supply — its own type, not a legal/illegal subcategory. See below.
  • Illegal — picking this opens a second menu (what it actually is: drug trafficking, chop shop, gun running, money laundering, or fencing) and a third (what it's fronted as, reusing the 7 legal types) — a laundromat that's really moving stolen cars, not a flat 9th option.

Public, Not Owner-Only

The Mining Depot

The one storefront mechanic that isn't gated to staff — any player can walk up to a registered Mining business and sell raw gathered materials (ore, coal, scrap, lumber, and more) straight to its depot. Payment draws from the business's own fund, so the owner needs real capital behind it to keep buying, and every sale is a genuine transaction between two players, not a system tap.

Running It

Biz Manager

The phone app for owners — dashboard, staff roster, hire/fire, set grades and wages, and review applicants who applied through LinkedUp.

💰

Society Funds

Every business has its own shared bank. Deposit, withdraw, and payroll pays staff automatically on a real timer — as long as the fund can actually cover it.

NPC Storefronts

Not player-owned — these are fixed vendor stations that already exist in the world regardless of anyone's economy, the shops you go to before you have a business of your own.

Pharmacy

On the Stock Market

Eight parody companies, publicly traded and player-tradeable through the phone's Stocks app. Prices drift on their own and get real nudges from gameplay — this is fictional in-world capital, not a business anyone owns directly.

TBPH — TBL Pharmaceuticals

Drug & pharma sector. Rises when street product moves.

TBSC — Tarboro Steel Corp

Mining and industrial. Tracks ore and smelter output.

VWAG — Vinewood Auto Group

Automotive. Rises with vehicle repairs and sales.

SSEN — Sandy Shores Energy

Fuel and raw materials. Tied to gathering and coal.

LSFN — LS Financial Holdings

Banking and money flows. Moves with laundering volume.

TBCO — Tarboro Construction

Lumber and building. Tracks mill output.

PSBS — Pacific Standard Bank

Institutional banking. Rises when crime rates fall.

BCTE — Blaine County Tech

Tech sector. Volatile — will tie into hacking systems.
